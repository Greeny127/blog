Resurrecting a Childhood PS2 Slim

Aug 20, 2026 1 min read ps2 · hardware · repair

Rust on the chassis, a dead AV cable, and a disc drive that just won't spin.

This is a PS2 Slim, model SCPH-75004, PAL region. The exact unit I had growing up, which never actually worked. The disc drive would spin up, seek, and just fail, over and over, in a grinding buzz that I now recognize as a spindle motor giving up. Opening the case turned up two separate problems instead of one:

  • Visible rust and corrosion on the internal metal chassis parts, which points to moisture exposure at some point in this thing's life
  • The disc lid button feels soft and doesn't rebound properly, which likely means the lid-detection switches aren't triggering fully — the console may think the lid is still open

On top of that, the console lost video output intermittently once I had the shell off, despite booting fine on the TV the night before. Tracing that back led to the AV Multi Out cable itself; it's missing several pins on the connector. The console's own port looked intact by comparison. It genuinely looked like the FNAF title screen glitches.

A replacement composite AV cable is on the way. Once that's in, I can rule the cable out properly and focus on the drive and the corrosion, which is really two repairs pretending to be one.
