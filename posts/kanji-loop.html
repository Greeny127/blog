The Two-Year Kanji Loop

Aug 2, 2026 1 min read Japanese · learning · language

How do I stick to my learning? Why is it so difficult to just spend half an hour a day, every day on learning kanji.

My first foray into learning kanji was through Anki. I thought it was the coolest thing in the world, the Spaced Repetition System - You're telling me this piece of software knows exactly when I'm just about to forget a particular set of information? Wow. Unfortunately, no matter how good your computer is, nothing can beat a lazy human. That human being me, of course. It always goes the same way: I get motivated → I do my reviews DAILY, without fail, and my lessons → I start losing steam, and "forget" to do it on some days → reviews start piling up so I stop doing lessons → loss of motivation, and I conveniently forget about the app altogether for 6 months → rinse and repeat. This has been me for the past 2 years. And because of my sheer stubbornness to restart every single time for continuity, I now know the first 20 beginner kanji like the back of my hand. Nothing more and nothing less. 2 years by the way.

So now, I'm trying WaniKani. Maybe the strictness and less-freedom plus a more polished experience will bring me to stick with it more. Well, at least for the levels past 2, because I'm sure I already know levels 1 and 2 by heart thanks to my shenanigans over the past two years.
